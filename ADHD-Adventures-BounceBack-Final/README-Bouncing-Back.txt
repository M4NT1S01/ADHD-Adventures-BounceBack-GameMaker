ADHD ADVENTURES: BOUNCING BACK

Open ADHD Adventures Bouncing Back.yyp in GameMaker and press F5.
The game starts directly in its one small room. There are no menu buttons.
Left/Right arrows move. Space jumps and begins or restarts the game.
Collect two focus stars and reach the gold finish in each of three rounds.
Spring pads launch the player and save checkpoints. Hazards return the player to the latest checkpoint.
